## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/LnQjpWaON8nhr21vNW/giphy.gif" width="28"> What I'm building

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ☀️ [SOLARIS](https://github.com/anxshh007/SOLARIS-solar-intelligence-for-the-living-grid)
`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `NumPy`

Explainable AI for distributed solar. The **SIF-X** engine fuses weather, generation, battery, grid and tariff data into one recommended action — with forecasting, panel-layout optimisation, battery-degradation tracking and net-metering workflows. Custom backprop in NumPy, so it runs offline without a GPU.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🪙 [ArthSetu](https://github.com/anxshh007/Behavioral-Analysis-of-Cashflow-for-Trust-Estimation.)
`TypeScript` `FinTech`

Financial inclusion for Bharat. Every rupee on every screen traces back to the transactions that produced it — transparency engine, unified dispute tracker, portable KYC credential and BACTE cashflow trust estimation. Real parsers, real training, simulated rails.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🌾 [Fasal Raksha](https://github.com/anxshh007/Fasal-Raksha-AI-Assisted-Farmer-Buyer-Matching-Platform)
`JavaScript` `Web Speech API` `Groq`

Farmer–buyer matching to stop crops rotting unsold. Voice input in **English, Hindi, Bengali and Punjabi**, match scoring on crop, price, quantity and distance — offline-first, runs entirely in the browser.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

Tic-Tac-Toe generalised into an *m,n,k* engine you play with your hands. Webcam gesture control, live win-probability distributions, alpha-beta pruning on 3×3 and heuristic MCTS on bigger boards.

</td>

</td>
